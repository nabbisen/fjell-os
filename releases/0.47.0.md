## What this release is

**Two lines and four small items. Five errata closed** — **E-126**, **E-127**, **E-128**, **E-129**,
all filed inside the milestone, and **E-012** after four months as an accepted limitation. The
register stands at **128 entries, 122 CLOSED, 6 ACCEPTED, 0 OPEN**: the fewest accepted limitations
this project has carried.

**0.47 is the milestone where the project's own statements became true**, and the first false statement
it corrected was the architect's. Scoping it began by verifying the prerequisite the 0.46 record had
written down for `DELEGATE_ONLY` — *"a single funnel for capability checks **and there is none**"* —
published in four places. **The funnel had existed since v0.2.0**, and its own module doc called it
*"the sole production authority-checking path"*. The real defect was the opposite shape and worse: it
declared itself sole and was not.

`consistency-check` is **27 subchecks** (25 at 0.46.0). The ABI moved **+14 items**, 545 → 559, with 0
removed and 0 changed. `unsafe` sites **298 → 291**. Host tests: lib **921**, bins **1610** (877 /
1558 at 0.46.0). The suite is **35 tiers**.

**There is no security advisory.** The kernel has never been distributed as a runnable artefact and
the product has not been placed on the market. **E-129 was a latent authority hole and not a leak** —
the distinction is drawn below, because it matters and it would be easy to overstate in either
direction.

## Exit criterion 8 — what it found

**One finding, and 0.47 did not cause it.** `docs/src/external-design/capability-lease.md` — a
**published** page — said `cap_bind_lease` *"replaces any earlier binding of that capability"*. **The
kernel refuses one:** a binding is **set once** (RFC-0.38-004), and `sys_cap_bind_lease` refuses a
capability that already carries one even to the same lease — stated at
`crates/fjell-cap/src/cspace.rs:181` and `crates/fjell-kernel/src/cap/syscall.rs:503`.

**The direction is what makes it more than stale.** It told a reader that an authority could be moved
off the lease governing it — which is **precisely what E-090 was filed and closed to prevent** — so
the page described the defect as the design, for nine milestones, in a document published to the web.

The same paragraph named only `LEASE_CREATE` where the call requires `LEASE_CREATE` **and**
`LEASE_BIND`, and it predated 0.47-001 R4, which replaced a hand-rolled CSpace scan with `require_held`.
All three corrected, dated, with the reasoning kept in the page.

**Swept rather than spot-fixed:** a tree-wide probe for any other live claim of binding replacement
returns nothing outside `docs/src/history/`, and `v1-limitations.md` row 9 already reads in the past
tense as the closed record of E-090.

**Two documents examined and deliberately left**, both prose about the past, which this project does
not rewrite: `docs/src/history/v0.2.x-dev-summary.md`, marked *"Historical — frozen at v0.2.x"*, whose
claim that all kernel syscalls *"now go through this path"* is **E-126's defect preserved at its
origin, by the milestone that built the funnel**; and
`docs/src/audit/capability-lease-enforcement-audit-v0.1.md`, a dated audit of v0.1.0/v0.1.2.

## What shipped

### RFC-0.47-001 — `require_cap` becomes the sole authority path, in fact (first line)

**Closes E-126 and E-129.** The funnel existed and was not sole. Three paths and three check orders:
`require_cap` through its wrapper (11 call sites) and directly (5 more); `check_right`, a second local
funnel serving the four IPC syscalls with **no kind and no scope check**; four inline copies in
`cap_copy`/`mint`/`revoke`/`inspect` testing the **lease before the right**; `sys_dma_alloc` deciding
kind inline and accepting a legacy alias; `sys_cap_bind_lease` deciding authority by **scanning** the
CSpace; and `sys_cap_delete` checking **nothing at all**.

One body behind three named entries — `require_cap` (kind mandatory), `require_cap_any_kind`,
`require_held` — now serves every one. **Deleted:** `check_right`, the four inline copies, the scan,
`build_msg`'s duplicate kind check, `sys_dma_alloc`'s dead branch, and the wrapper's second
`CapError`→`SysError` table. `cap/syscall.rs` holds **0** `rights.contains` and **0** `check_lease`
sites.

**The design question the handoff asked was answered, not dodged.** A sentinel "any kind" value in a
mandatory parameter was the wrong shape; `Option<CapKind>` *inside* one body with **two public names**
keeps a kind-agnostic call visible and greppable at the call site. That is the implementer's reasoning
and it is better than the question.

### The three ABI-visible changes, taken deliberately

| Class | Before | After | Syscalls |
|---|---|---|---|
| **order** — lacks the right **and** lease revoked | `LeaseRevoked` | `PermissionDenied` | `cap_copy`, `cap_mint`, `cap_revoke`, `cap_inspect` |
| **kind vs right** — non-endpoint capability lacking the IPC right | `PermissionDenied` | `WrongType` | `ipc_send`, `ipc_call` |
| **kind** — non-endpoint capability **holding** the IPC right | *accepted as an endpoint `object_id`* | `WrongType` | `ipc_recv`, `ipc_try_recv` |

**`PermissionDenied` is right on a disclosure argument, not a consistency one:** telling a caller that
lacks the right *at all* that a lease was revoked discloses the state of an object it has no authority
over. The normative order merely agrees. **The canonical `to_sys_error` is now the only map**, so a
stale handle answers `GenerationMismatch` and an empty slot `SlotEmpty` through every path; the
minimal-churn alternative was rejected because it would have required amending RFC 031 §2.7's
documented table to match code, **which is the inversion this milestone exists to end**.

### E-129 — the receive path never checked a capability's kind

Found by the implementer answering an open question in the handoff, **demonstrated on a real boot**,
and **confirmed independently and statically by the architect** before it was accepted.
`sys_ipc_recv` and `sys_ipc_try_recv` took `object_id` from whatever capability carried the `RECV`
bit. `RECV` is bit 4, inside `ALL_NON_META`, and the tree grants `ALL_NON_META` to `LeaseAdmin`,
`TaskCreate`, `TaskControl` and `Reboot` capabilities **whose `object_id` is 0** — so all four polled
endpoint 0 through a capability that is not an endpoint, returning `WouldBlock` where `WrongType` was
the only defensible answer.

**It was not a leak**, because nothing sends to endpoint 0 — and **that accident is what held the
invariant**, not the kernel. `object-zero`, the subcheck gating *"no task can receive on endpoint
object 0"*, reads **`Endpoint` installs only** and was green throughout; its scope statement is
corrected too, because a gate that states more than it checks is half of this defect.

**A fifth capability (`DmaRegion`) was in that run and its result was lost in the serial interleave.
It is not claimed.** That refusal is the most reviewable thing in the milestone: an unclaimed fifth is
worth more than a fifth asserted from a run nobody could read.

### RFC-0.47-002 — the lifecycle policy's kept rules are written down (second line)

**Closes E-128**, whose diagnosis was wrong when filed and whose correction matters more than the
original claim. `RFC-0.25-002`'s D1 kept five things from the in-repo policy. **They landed:**
`be8cf6f` put the merged RFC 000 at 430 lines containing the drift rule, and its own commit message
records all five *"verified independently by name … verbatim as a normative blockquote"*.

**Then `4d8044a` — 2026-10-21, entire commit message "RFC 000 life cycle policy: revised", no body —
deleted every one of them:**

```
in RFC 000                                 before   after
the drift rule ("may not be marked")            1       0
Implemented-with-Errata                        13       0
the **Accepted** table row                      1       0
Closed                                          7       0
Test plan (the required-sections list)           1       0
NNN-slug (the portable document's shape)         2      16
```

It reinstated the general-purpose shape RFC-0.25-002 had analysed and rejected with a table of
consequences. **No erratum. It stood two months**, during which **every disposition in the errata
register applied a rule citable from no normative document.**

RFC 000 now defines six states with the drift rule **inside the definition of `Implemented`** — a
condition on a state's meaning belongs where the term is defined — with the discovery clause the
original lacked. **`status.rs` parses that table** instead of holding a vocabulary, so an instrument
can no longer make a word legal that the policy does not define.

**RFC-0.25-002 keeps `Implemented`.** Its normative text *was* satisfied when it shipped;
`Implemented-with-Errata` records that an RFC claimed more than the tree delivered, and marking it
would record a false fact and misuse the rule being restored.

### The two new gates, and what they caught

**`authority-funnel`** (27th subcheck) fails an authority check outside the funnel and — the half
`unsafe-audit` cannot make — a `SAFETY` comment claiming `require_cap` validated a handle in a
function that reaches no funnel entry. Run against the kernel as it stood before the fix it reports
**26 findings**, including **exactly the seven false SAFETY claims at lines 35, 83, 146, 187, 467, 504
and 923** — the same set the architect had derived by hand at stage 1, by a different method.
**Two independent methods agreeing on one set of seven lines is the evidence it works on the real
defect rather than on fixtures**, and the architect re-ran it in a scratch worktree at stage 2 rather
than take the claim.

**It caught what nobody had:** `sys_cap_install` — written in 0.46, by the author of E-126's false
claim — had itself hand-rolled the funnel's steps 1–3 and 7. **The line that claimed to be the one
place authority is decided had missed one in the line written just before it.**

**`rfc-policy`** (26th subcheck) checks the vocabulary three ways, the rule's presence **by name** at
both ends — *the half RFC-0.25-002's own checklist asked for and never got* — that every
`Implemented-with-Errata` RFC is named in the register, and the forward `- **RFC:**` field. It also
exposed two defects in the instruments themselves: `rfc-status-folder` treated a Status naming **no
defined state** as *"no Status field"* and skipped it, and `Implemented-with-Errata` **matched its own
prefix `Implemented`**, so deleting the state would have silently re-read sixteen RFCs as plain
`Implemented`.

**The restored rule's first harvest: four RFCs re-marked `Implemented-with-Errata`** —
`RFC-v0.12-002` (E-004), `RFC-0.41-002` (E-129), `RFC-0.33-003` (E-073), `RFC-v0.7.4-003` (E-093).
The count rises 12 → **16**. **Three of the four predate the deletion, so the rule was not being
applied while it was still written down** — which is why a written rule needed a check.

## Known limitations at this release

**128 entries, 6 accepted, 0 open**, every accepted erratum referenced in `v1-limitations.md`.

* **`DELEGATE_ONLY` is still not taken** — now for a stated reason rather than a false one. A negative
  right is a design smell that deserves its own RFC against a `Delegable` kind or an install-source
  table.
* **E-093 — an unbound capability's copies have no recursive revocation at all.** Parent links were
  deleted on the premise that every meaningful capability has a lease; nothing enforces it. Deferred
  to 0.48 on sequencing, and **its subject RFC now says so in its own status**.
* **E-073 — two `#[repr(C)]` wire layouts are described by nothing a gate compares**, and closing
  `AuditRecordBin` honestly means changing where the kernel writes every audit record.
* **E-095 — three of the measurement chain's four events are `0xAB` placeholders**, because nothing
  verifies a release, a rootfs or a policy bundle.
* **E-122 — the v0.4 network plane is three non-functional images.**
* **E-004 — no hardware has ever been booted.**
* **The 37 mismatched `SAFETY` justifications are unstarted.** Of the 50 `unsafe` sites taking a
  static-mut reference, **37 carry a justification that does not describe that operation** and 19 the
  wrong `category=` tag — verified by reading three, including one inside `require_cap_on_ct` itself.
  `unsafe-audit` reports 291 sites with 291 comments because it checks that a comment *exists*.
  **Whether this becomes a line is the owner's call.**
* **The readiness matrix's "20 obligations" matches none of the four figures `verus-count` states.**

**No accepted-risk statement is written for this release.** No gate that should have blocked was left
unpassed.
