## What this release is

Four RFCs, **five errata closed** (**E-085**, **E-086**, **E-088**, **E-089**,
**E-090**). The kernel hashes the 33 service images it embeds and hands `init`
the digest, so one of the measurement chain's four recorded events is now a fact
about the bytes that booted, checked against an independent host recomputation
for equality. `sys_cap_bind_lease` performs the authority check its
documentation always claimed and a binding can no longer be replaced;
`sys_ipc_try_recv` stops stranding the sender that fed it, and the console
delivers more than one byte per boot.

**What still does not hold, in the same breath:** three of the chain's four
events are still the literal `0xAB` (**E-095**); the signed attestation record is
still independent constants and does not capture the chain (**E-094**); the chain
measures service images only; and `measurement_head_available` still reads
`false` — `DIAGNOSTICSD` slot 4 stays uninstalled, which is 0.39's.

## What shipped

### RFC-0.38-001 — the measurement chain measures something

The kernel hashes every embedded service image at boot and folds the per-image
digests into one image-set digest
(`SHA-256("FJELL-IMAGE-SET-V1" ‖ u16 count ‖ Σ(u16 image_id ‖ SHA-256(image)))`),
writes it into a read-only `BootInfo` v2 page in `init` (address in `a1`), and
`init` hands it to `measuredd` with the new `APPEND_IMAGE_SET` op. `measuredd`
fixes that event's kind, source and subject. `GET_HEAD` returns the whole 32-byte
chain digest; `diagnosticsd`'s head query is a real call (it never could have
worked before: it sent one way and read from `measuredd`'s own endpoint). A new
`measurement` tier demands that both serial digest lines equal a host
recomputation made with an external SHA-256 — shown failing on the pre-change
tree, on an image altered after the kernel was built, and on a digest corrupted
in transit. Hashing costs about 3 ms of boot. Closes **E-085**, **E-086**; the
published `fjell_abi::boot::BootInfo` changed shape (`### Breaking` in the
CHANGELOG), and `fjell-syscall` gained `sys_ipc_call4`, a wrapper over the
existing `IpcCall`.

### RFC-0.38-002 — a lease binding needs the authority it claims

`sys_cap_bind_lease` now requires an active `LeaseAdmin` capability carrying
`LEASE_CREATE` (`PermissionDenied` otherwise) and refuses a revoked lease
(`LeaseRevoked`) — the check its documentation described, reverted earlier over
an aliasing concern and never reinstated. Closes **E-088**.

### RFC-0.38-003 — a non-blocking receive must not strand its sender

`sys_ipc_try_recv` neither woke a one-way sender nor installed a call's reply
edge, so the UART driver never acknowledged its interrupt and the console
delivered one byte per boot. Both receives now share one completion path. Closes
**E-089**.

### RFC-0.38-004 — a lease binding is set once

A capability that already has a lease binding refuses a second bind (`BadState`),
to another lease or the same one, so the cascading revocation a lease promises
cannot be opted out of by the party being revoked. Closes **E-090** (rebind half);
**E-091** is untouched.

## What this release does not claim

- **That the measurement chain attests the system.** One event of four is a real
  measurement, of the embedded service images only — not the kernel image, not
  configuration, rootfs or runtime state. The other three record `0xAB`
  (**E-095**).
- **That an attestation record says anything about the chain.** `attestd` signs
  constants and holds no route to `measuredd` (**E-094**); the signature is the
  development keyed-SHA-256 provider.
- **That a measurement head is available to diagnostics.** `GET_HEAD` now returns
  the whole digest, shown end to end with a scratch capability and reverted, but
  `DIAGNOSTICSD` slot 4 stays uninstalled and `measurement_head_available` reads
  `false` — 0.39's.
- **That a bundle can leave the node**, unchanged since 0.37.0.
- **That `fjell-audit-format`'s or `fjell-net-format`'s on-wire layouts have a
  generated description** (**E-073**).
- **That a clean dependency check describes what Fjell OS ships.** The published
  crates and the kernel have no third-party dependencies; the check covers tools,
  tests, benchmarks and the development-grade crypto crate, and says so itself.
- **That CI gates the release.** Criteria 9 and 11 *record* CI; every gate runs
  locally.
- **Cross-machine reproducibility**, as at every previous release.
