# Architect Review — v0.18.3 Verus Layer — Decisions Record

**Date:** 2026-06-04
**Subject:** Verus verification implementation v0.17.0–v0.18.3
**Outcome:** Approved with conditions before v1.0.0

---

## D1 — Approve v0.18.3 Verus implementation

**Decision: Approved.**

The Verus adoption is confirmed as a valid verification foundation. The
proof layer is narrowly scoped, non-invasive to ordinary Rust development,
and release-gated only for the chosen invariants.

---

## D2 — Ratify capability and lease as release-required

**Decision: Ratified.**

Both targets are promoted to `release_required = true`. The release gate
(`cargo xtask verus-check --release-required`) must pass before any release
of Fjell OS. Conformance-only is not sufficient for a release-required target.

---

## D3 — Two-milestone promotion timing

**Decision: Ratified by explicit exception.**

*Exception text (to be cited in all future promotion reviews):*

> The policy intent (proof surviving natural code-churn across at least two
> releases) was only partially satisfied: both recorded PASS milestones
> (v0.17.1, v0.18.0) landed in a single continuous development session
> rather than across real intervening development. The architect accepts the
> promotion because the proofs are machine-checked, conformance-backed,
> property-tested, and release-gated. Future promotions must require natural
> code-churn survival unless explicitly waived by a new architect exception.

This exception is recorded in the errata register as ACCEPTED.

---

## D4 — R-V1 rule

**Decision: Ratified.**

The R-V1 rule is confirmed as standing policy:

> A Verus machine-check failure cannot be overridden by passing Rust tests.
> A release-required target remains uncertified unless the prover runs and
> the proof succeeds. Property tests and conformance tests are valuable
> evidence but are not a substitute for a machine-checked proof.

---

## D5 — Boot-control promotion

**Decision: Not immediately; schedule for the next release cycle.**

Boot-control (`select_bcb_mirror`) is machine-checked (7 obligations, 0 errors)
and its invariant is security-critical. The architect endorses keeping it at
pilot-required (`release_required = false`, tier 2) for v0.18.3 to avoid
repeating the D3 timing pattern. It should be promoted after one additional
release rehearsal or one small development cycle.

**Scheduled:** promotion to `release_required = true` at the next tag
following a natural development cycle, recorded in `proof-gate-policy.md`.

---

## D6 — v1.0.0 tag

**Decision: Not yet approved.**

The v1.0.0 tag requires:
1. RFC-v0.17-001 trust-anchor provisioning decision (see below — now recorded).
2. Gate 9 manual sign-off by the owner.
3. At least one real QEMU negative test for a capability or lease refusal path
   (satisfied by v0.19.0: capability profile passes 8 real markers including
   `NEG:CAP:MINT_WITHOUT_RIGHT_REJECTED:PASS`).

## Trust-anchor provisioning decision (§4.4 / RFC-v0.17-001)

The architect's decision for RFC-v0.17-001 §4 and §6:

| Profile | Mechanism | Condition |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Development / QEMU | TOFU first-boot | Only with explicit `--allow-tofu-provision` flag; not silent default |
| Factory / field v1.1 | Factory station provisioning | — |
| High-assurance v2+ | Hardware-anchored provisioning | — |

*Do not allow silent default TOFU for any v1-supported profile.*

This decision is recorded in RFC-v0.17-001 (moved to `rfcs/done/`) and closes
item 6 in `docs/release/v1-limitations.md`.
